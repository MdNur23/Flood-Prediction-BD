# Research Questions

## Research Title

AI-Based Multi-Source 24-Hour Flood Risk Prediction System for Bangladesh Using Deep Learning

## Main Research Question

Can a deep learning model accurately predict flood risk in Bangladesh 24 hours in advance using rainfall, river water level, and weather data?

## Research Objectives

1. Collect multi-source flood-related data.
2. Preprocess and clean the data.
3. Develop an LSTM-based prediction model.
4. Predict flood risk 24 hours ahead.
5. Compare model performance with baseline methods.
6. Evaluate the model using standard performance metrics.
