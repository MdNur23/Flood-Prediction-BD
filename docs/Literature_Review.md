## Research Questions

### Main Research Question
Can an AI-based multi-source LSTM model accurately predict flood risk in Bangladesh 24 hours in advance using rainfall, river water level, and weather data?

## Research Objectives

1. Collect rainfall, river level, and weather data for Bangladesh.
2. Preprocess and clean the collected data.
3. Develop an LSTM-based flood prediction model.
4. Predict flood risk 24 hours in advance.
5. Compare the model with other machine learning algorithms.
6. Evaluate the performance using standard metrics.
